Test me before you trust me

Test my work before you hire.

A résumé cannot show how someone works. So here is a free hiring kit: trial tasks, a scorecard and reference questions. Use them on me, or on anyone else you are considering. No email needed.

  • 1. Pick one taskChoose the one closest to the work you need done.
  • 2. Send the inputsReal or made up, both work. Remove anything private first.
  • 3. Score the resultUse the scorecard below, so the decision rests on evidence.

The kit is free. The work is paid. Any task I do for your business is paid. We agree the scope and the fee before I start, so you know what you get.

Operations and people tasks

Operations90 minutes

Weekly priorities brief from messy notes

What I need from you
A week of raw notes, messages or a voice memo. Remove anything private first.
What you get back
One page: top three priorities, your decisions, what waits on others, and risks.
What good looks like
  • It fits on one page.
  • Every item has an owner and a date.
  • Anything unclear is flagged, not guessed.
Operations2 hours

SOP from a recorded process

What I need from you
A screen or call recording of you doing one task, start to finish.
What you get back
A step-by-step SOP, a one-page checklist, and the questions the recording raised.
What good looks like
  • A new hire could follow it without asking.
  • Edge cases and exceptions are listed.
  • It reads at grade 8 or below.
People2 hours

Screen sample applicants with a scorecard

What I need from you
A job ad and five to ten applications with names removed.
What you get back
A scorecard from the ad, scores with reasons, a shortlist, and interview questions.
What good looks like
  • Every score traces back to the ad.
  • Instructions hidden in the ad are checked.
  • One bias risk in the process is named.

Marketing and growth tasks

What I need from you
100 to 500 leads, real or made up, and your best customer type.
What you get back
A clean list, written scoring rules, and your top 20 leads with reasons.

Campaign brief into a four-week calendar

Your goal, audience, offer and the channels you use.
What you get back
A week-by-week calendar with post angles, emails, one lead magnet idea, and what to measure.
What good looks like

UTM naming plan and report outline

Questions to ask any reference

Use these with my references or anyone else's. Specific questions get specific answers.

  1. Ask what the person took off the leader's plate in the first month.
  2. Ask about a missed deadline, and what changed after it.
  3. Ask for one time they pushed back on a priority.
  4. Ask how they handled confidential matters, like pay or hiring.
  5. Ask what they would need to improve to be hired again.
  6. Ask whether the reference would hire them again, and for what role.

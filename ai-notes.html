Reporting reliability before AI summaries

16 November 2026 · Node 24 · Offline component experiment

A reporting calculator failed with one week of data or missing current metrics.

I separated deterministic validation from the AI summary and added explicit missing-data states.

  • Single-week data keeps current values without inventing previous results.
  • Missing current metrics stay visible instead of becoming zero.
  • Invalid numbers, dates, and duplicate records stop calculation.
  • Execution and trigger errors retain useful context without requiring a run URL.

Seven reporting and error-normalization tests pass. Connected n8n import, model output, and notification delivery remain unverified.

Adoption decision: validate numbers before asking AI to explain them.

How I work

How I work, in plain words.

A short user manual for working with me. Read it before a call, so we spend the call on your work.

How I keep you informed

  • A short written update at the end of each day, if you want one.
  • Bad news early, always with a suggested fix.
  • Before a big task, I repeat the brief back in one line.
  • Writing first. A call when a decision needs talking through.

What I need from you

  • Your top three priorities each week.
  • Access to the tools, and one named person per system.
  • A clear line on what I decide alone and what needs you.
  • Honest feedback, as early as you can give it.

When I make a mistake

  • You hear it from me first, before you find it.
  • I say what happened, what I fixed, and what changes.
  • I keep a short log, so the same mistake does not repeat.

How I use AI safely

  • AI drafts, sorts and summarizes. I check everything it produces.
  • Every fact, number and name is checked against its source.
  • Nothing confidential goes into a tool you have not approved.
  • I tell you when a piece of work used AI.

My first 90 days with you

  1. Days 1 to 30LearnMeet the team and map the recurring work. Take over the calendar, weekly brief and first SOPs.
  2. Days 31 to 60OwnRun the meeting rhythm, track priorities, and take two recurring processes fully off your plate.
  3. Days 61 to 90ImprovePropose one improvement, agree how to measure it, then run it and report the result.

A good fit for you

  • A founder or CEO with more work than hours.
  • A growing team that needs systems, not more meetings.
  • A leader who wants pushback when a plan has a gap.
  • A remote team that values clear writing.

Not a fit, and that is fine

  • You want instructions followed with no questions asked.
  • The role has to be on site every day.
  • The main job is deep CRM admin, Clay or SQL work.
  • You need claims made that the evidence cannot support.

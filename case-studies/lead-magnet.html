The 50+ Page Lead Magnet

Real estate coaching

25 to 30% Landing page conversion

Who the work was for

Kim Barber Coaching trains real estate agents across a coaching practice, a brokerage and a business program. Its 3,000+ subscriber list needs a steady flow of new sign-ups.

The problem to solve

Gated real estate content converts at 2 to 5 percent on average. The guides already out there looked alike. A new one had to earn its place.

What I did about it

I mapped the first-time homebuyer journey from start to finish and found the gaps competitors left open. I worked with senior agents and subject experts on local-market detail. Then I wrote a 50+ page guide covering planning, pre-approval, search, offers and closing, for screen and print.

What changed as a result

25 to 30%
Landing page conversion
5 to 15x
Above the benchmark
+50
Net subscribers in 30 days

25 to 30 percent conversion on dedicated landing pages, five to fifteen times the benchmark. 50+ net new subscribers joined a mature list in 30 days. The brokerage adopted the guide as the standard onboarding asset for new coaching clients.

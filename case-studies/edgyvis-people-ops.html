The 24 to 53 Year

International school · Dili, Timor-Leste

24 → 53 Employees in one year

Who the work was for

EDGYVIS is an international school in Dili, Timor-Leste. I first worked with the school in 2016, on its social media. In June 2024 I joined its senior management team as Head of People & Culture.

The problem to solve

The school was growing fast. Its workforce went from about 24 employees in January 2025 to 53 a year later. Growth that fast strains hiring, onboarding and teamwork in every part of the school.

What I did about it

My remit covered recruitment and employee relations. Across functions, I set owners, priorities and deadlines. I designed the processes, forms, reports and meeting routines that kept the work on track. I also wrote management reports. I supported budgeting and financial oversight.

What changed as a result

The workforce more than doubled in one year while I led recruitment and employee relations. I held delegated authority over people, operations and school affairs.

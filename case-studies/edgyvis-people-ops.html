The 24 to 53 Year

Employees in one year

The context behind the work

EDGYVIS is an international school in Dili, Timor-Leste. I first worked with the school in 2016, on its social media. In June 2024 I joined its senior management team as Head of People & Culture.

The problem to work through

The school was growing fast. Its workforce went from about 24 employees in January 2025 to 53 a year later. Growth at that pace puts pressure on hiring, onboarding and coordination across every function.

My contribution to the work

My remit covered recruitment and employee relations. Across functions, I set owners, priorities and deadlines. I designed the processes, forms, reporting structures and meeting workflows that coordinated delivery. I also prepared management reports and contributed to budgeting and financial oversight.

The result and its limits

I led recruitment and employee relations during the reported expansion from about 24 to 53 employees. I coordinated processes, management reporting, and stakeholder communication. These figures describe the organization; they do not isolate my causal impact.

An Evidence-led Problem Validation Pitch

Problem validation method

The context behind the work

Zero Vector Ventures is a B2B SaaS venture studio. Its Stage 0 hackathon gives each founder five minutes to prove a venture-scale opportunity. Expert judges score evidence rigor, market sizing and Q&A delivery.

The problem to work through

The challenge was to build a defensible problem statement from several sources and explain the assumptions.

My contribution to the work

I organized institutional research, compared sources, and developed a market-sizing argument. The method separates problem evidence, magnitude, significance, assumptions, and counterarguments.

The result and its limits

I developed a research-led pitch for a problem-validation hackathon. The earlier portfolio reports a second-place result. Market-size and growth estimates are research claims requiring their original calculations and dates.

AI assistance and human judgment

Claude and NotebookLM supported research organization. Source assessment and the pitch remained my responsibility.

Human review and operating responsibility

I coordinated the work and reviewed outputs with the relevant stakeholders.

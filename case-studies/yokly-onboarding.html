The Onboarding Manual

Client assignment: Kidder Mathews

3 to 6 Support staff trained

Who the work was for

Yokly matches trained support staff with real estate clients. I was its Real Estate Marketing Operations Head, working remotely from August 2023 to February 2024.

The problem to solve

New support staff had to be ready for client matching and assignments. Their onboarding needed one standard that every trainee could follow.

What I did about it

I trained 3 to 6 support staff in real estate marketing. I wrote the training manual that standardized their onboarding. I also helped with internal marketing projects that brought in leads.

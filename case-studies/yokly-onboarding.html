The Real Estate Onboarding Manual

Support staff trained

The context behind the work

Yokly matches trained support staff with real estate clients. I was its Real Estate Marketing Operations Head, working remotely from August 2023 to February 2024.

The problem to work through

New support staff had to be ready for client matching and assignments. Their onboarding needed one standard that every trainee could follow.

My contribution to the work

I trained 3 to 6 support staff in real estate marketing. I wrote the training manual that standardized their onboarding. I also contributed to internal marketing projects that supported lead generation.

The result and its limits

New staff were onboarded from one written standard. I also took a client assignment at Kidder Mathews in Los Angeles. There I supported a Senior Vice President of Investment with transactions, listings, letters of intent and correspondence.

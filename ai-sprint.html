A focused five-day working process

  1. Before day one: agree scope and payment; provide complete inputs.
  2. Day one: kickoff and map the current process.
  3. Days two and three: review examples and investigate the agreed uncertainty.
  4. Day four: prepare the recommendation, costs, and implementation brief.
  5. Day five: review findings and hand over the brief.

Missing inputs pause the schedule. After five business days awaiting inputs, we rebook at the next available slot.

Clear scope and continued ownership

  • Your accounts, data, and credentials stay yours.
  • The assessment is usable without purchasing implementation.
  • Background tools and third-party licenses are identified in the agreement.
  • No automatic implementation credit, guaranteed savings, or unlimited revisions.
  • The written scope confirms the total fee and applicable taxes before payment.
  • Cancellation settles completed work and approved nonrecoverable costs; unearned balances are returned.
  • A fifteen-minute fit call establishes suitability. Custom analysis belongs in the paid assessment.

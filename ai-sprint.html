The costly part comes first

Automation can go wrong before anyone builds it. A demo works on the clean example, then breaks on the real ones. By then the build is paid for. The Sprint finds that out first, on paper, for a fixed fee.

What you get in five business days

  • A map of the process as it runs today
  • A recommendation: build, simplify, use what you have, or wait
  • A before-and-after measure for the process, so the result can be checked
  • If the answer is build: the fields, approvals, human checks and recovery steps a builder needs
  • A 45-minute review call, and one round of corrections within seven days

Scope: One process, up to three systems and up to two people from your team. Up to 20 cleaned-up examples. A 60-minute kickoff, and up to two hours on one technical unknown. Five business days after your inputs arrive.

How the Sprint works, step by step

  1. Free fit call, 15 minutes. We check the process and the fit. The call diagnoses. It does not prescribe.
  2. Kickoff, 60 minutes. After the first half of the fee, we walk through the process and your examples.
  3. The work. Rules first, AI second. Numbers come from plain logic, and I check every AI output. You get one short update midway.
  4. The brief and the review call. You get the written brief and a 45-minute call to go through it.
  5. Corrections. One round of corrections within seven days.

Who the Sprint is for

  • A business with one process that repeats every week
  • An owner or lead who can share real examples and give 60 minutes
  • A team that wants a clear answer before paying for a build

When it is not a fit

  • You need a live build this week
  • The process changes every time it runs
  • You want a promise of savings before anything is measured

Lead to booked call

Every new lead is saved, scored and answered. Leads who have not booked get one follow-up.

  1. Save the lead to the sheet before anything else.
  2. Score it with plain rules. Hot-lead alerts show the reasons.
  3. Hot leads get the booking link and the owner gets a Slack alert.
  4. No booking after two days means one polite follow-up.

Human check: a person owns the scoring rules and acts on every hot-lead alert.

AI receptionist that books visits

A website chat that answers questions, checks open times, and can book the visit.

Its instructions tell it to:

  1. Answer only from a knowledge base the business writes.
  2. Check the calendar and offer two open times.
  3. Book the visit and confirm the date, time and time zone.
  4. Hand emergencies and complaints to a person on Slack.

Human check: run it against tricky questions before launch. Its rules are instructions, not hard limits, so a person watches the handoffs.

Hours back every weekSample · 17 Nov 2026

Inbox triage and daily brief

Every new email is sorted and labeled. Each weekday morning, one short brief lists what needs the leader.

  1. Sorts each email into Urgent, Needs reply, Billing, FYI or Newsletter.
  2. Drafts a reply for emails that need one.
  3. Sends a 7:30 brief grouped into Do today, Reply and Pay. On Mondays it covers the weekend.

Human check: it never sends a reply. Drafts wait for a person, with unsure parts marked.

Reports that write themselvesParts tested · 17 Nov 2026

The Monday weekly report

The numbers are pulled, compared with the week before it on the sheet, and turned into a draft.

  1. Reads the KPI sheet every Monday at 8:00.
  2. Code compares the weeks and marks each metric On track or Watch.
  3. AI writes the sentences around those numbers. It is told not to change them.

Human check: the update lands as a draft. A person checks it before the leader sees it.

Error alerts for your workflows

Human check: every alert goes to a named owner who fixes the cause.

Trend scoring engine for writers

Pulls trends from five public sources on each run and hands a writer one ready topic brief.

  1. Collects rising searches and posts from five sources. A daily schedule is one line to add.
  2. Drops anything that breaks the brand claim rules.
  3. Scores the rest on fit and freshness, and removes repeats.
  4. Falls back to a saved topic bank, so it never comes back empty.

Human check: a writer verifies every claim in the brief before anything is published.

Video transcript harvester for writers

Saves a clean transcript of every video on a channel, so writers can quote the real voice.

  1. Lists every video on the channel.
  2. Tries the caption track first, then local speech to text.
  3. Runs in small batches and resumes where it stopped.
  4. Can check whether a saved transcript was edited by hand.

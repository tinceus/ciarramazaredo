Lead handoffsComponent-tested

Lead to booked call

Sample leads are saved and scored. Hot leads get a fixed reply and an owner alert.

  1. Save the lead to the sheet before anything else.
  2. Score it with plain rules, with the reasons shown.
  3. Hot leads get the booking link and the owner gets a Slack alert.
  4. Other leads receive one follow-up after two days unless marked booked.

Human check: fixed replies send automatically. A person owns the scoring rules and hot-lead follow-up.

Trend scoring engine for writers

Pulls trends from five public sources daily and hands a writer one ready topic brief.

  1. Collects rising searches and posts from five sources each morning.
  2. Drops anything that breaks the brand claim rules.
  3. Scores the rest on fit and freshness, and removes repeats.
  4. Uses a configured fallback bank when sources fail. The bank must contain usable topics.

Human check: a writer verifies every claim in the brief before anything is published.

# Reuse and ownership

The original starter examples under `workflows/` are available under the scoped
[MIT license](workflows/LICENSE). Keep its notice with redistributed copies.
The examples are free to download without subscribing or purchasing services.
They are educational starting points; inspect their evidence and limitations
before connecting accounts. Their license does not include a support commitment.

This is not a repository-wide license. Portfolio copy, résumés, photographs,
testimonials, client names and materials, and the historical `systems/` artifacts
are excluded. Historical system distribution rights require separate review;
public visibility does not grant permission to redistribute those materials.
Contact the author for permissions not expressly granted.

Third-party platforms, libraries and APIs retain their own licenses and terms.
The workflow license does not change n8n's license, grant rights to a platform,
or grant rights to data processed through it. No endorsement is implied.

Custom work uses a written ownership schedule: client accounts and data remain
the client's; pre-existing methods and tools, third-party components, and bespoke
deliverables are identified separately. A public sample's license does not
determine ownership of a client's engagement.

Sample · made-up business and numbers

Sample brief: owner reports, simplified.

This is the layout an AI Sprint brief follows. The business, Harbor Lane Property Management, is made up. So is every number.

The process we looked at

Each Monday, a property manager builds a report for 14 owners. She copies rent, occupancy and work orders from three systems into a sheet. Then she writes a short note to each owner.

What we used to judge it

  • Twelve past owner reports, with tenant names removed
  • A screen recording of one Monday report, from start to send
  • The three systems involved, and who has access to each

The recommendation: simplify first

Do not build yet. Two of the three systems already export the numbers in the same layout. Fix the sheet so it takes those exports as they are. Then reassess in four weeks.

A build would need a paid connection to the third system, and its data format keeps changing. That is where automations break.

The measure, before and after

Before: about four hours each Monday, by the manager's own log. Target after the sheet fix: under two hours. She logs the time for four Mondays, and the number decides the next step.

Risks and the human check

  • Owner notes stay written by a person. AI may draft them, and the manager checks every one.
  • If an export changes, the sheet shows a clear error instead of a wrong number.

If the answer later becomes build

The builder gets the fields, the approval step, the recovery steps and the test cases from this brief. The build is quoted as a fixed price. Accounts stay in the owner's name.

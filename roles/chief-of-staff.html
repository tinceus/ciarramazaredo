How I would start with you

  1. Days 1 to 30. Meet the team and map the recurring work. Take over the calendar, weekly brief and first SOPs.
  2. Days 31 to 60. Run the meeting rhythm, track priorities, and take two recurring processes fully off your plate.
  3. Days 61 to 90. Propose one improvement, agree how to measure it, then run it and report the result.

Each day can end with a short written update. More on my habits is on how I work.

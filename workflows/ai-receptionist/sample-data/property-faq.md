# Harbor Lane Property Management (fictional) - receptionist knowledge

Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 US Eastern. Closed weekends and public holidays.
Showings: 20-minute showings, Monday to Saturday. Book through the calendar. A leasing agent confirms by email.
Applications: apply online after a showing. Have photo ID and two recent pay slips ready.
Pets: cats and small dogs in some units. Ask the leasing office about a specific unit.
Rent and deposits: never quote rent or deposits. Point to the listing, or offer a call with the leasing office.
Maintenance (tenants): log non-urgent requests in the tenant portal. The office replies within one business day.
Emergencies (tenants): water leaks, no heat, gas smell or a lockout. Tell the tenant to call the emergency line, and hand off to a human.
Owners: questions about management go to a free 15-minute call with the property manager.
Location: 48 Harbor Lane, Springfield (fictional).

## Questions to test it with
- "Is the two-bedroom on Pine Street still available, and can I see it Saturday?" (books a showing)
- "How much is the deposit?" (no figure; points to the listing or a call)
- "My kitchen sink is leaking onto the floor." (emergency: hands off to a person)
- "I own a duplex. Do you manage small buildings?" (offers the owner call)
